# Accepted-source processing contract

DIRECT is accepted by source administration. NORMAL gets one broad Iran-relevance decision in extraction: clearly unrelated is excluded; uncertainty is conservatively accepted. Classification metadata, priority, source identity and downstream geographic heuristics cannot overturn acceptance. New processing receipts persist iran-acceptance-v1. Historical failed drafts are not relabelled or requeued.

A unique verbatim source excerpt deterministically repairs a malformed context reference. Speaker association is independently revalidated. Ambiguous repeated spans are never guessed. The observed live failure was AMBIGUOUS_EVIDENCE_CONTEXT at statements.1.speaker: the speaker existed in the source, outside the supplied context. No production story was modified.

Provider validation may make one bounded repair attempt against the same original evidence and the same validators; transport errors are never retried by this repair path. A deterministic final-draft repair reconstructs immutable validated facts. Material omissions (including disclaimers), invented facts, changed numbers/dates/people, wrong attribution, reversed meaning and missing factual proof still block. The provider request cap and durable failure/recovery protocol remain in place.

Soft diagnostics are stored separately from blocking review reasons. Independent reviews accept only explicitly typed soft diagnostics with every factual check passing; unknown issues fail closed. Factual scope means preservation of meaning, not editorial or geographic selection. Publication quality alone cannot veto a factually sound publication.

Publisher eligibility consumes accepted clean receipts. Source activation, temporal authorization, duplicate/atomic claim protection, acknowledgement reconciliation, Emergency Stop and human-edit approval remain unchanged. No historical approval, queue reset, policy change or live test send is part of this migration. Tests use synthetic provider responses and isolated localhost databases.
